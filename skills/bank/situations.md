# Situations

This is the one lookup. Find the decision you face in the tag list, go to its section, and follow
the links into the breakdown sections. Measured plays come first under every tag, then Reported,
then Opinion. A `Conflict:` line means two plays under that tag disagree; weigh both, never pick
one silently. Every play is a recorded claim from its speaker, not an endorsement.

## Tags

- `finding-leads`: where prospects come from, lists, sourcing, lead magnets that make buyers raise a hand
- `cold-outreach`: cold email and DMs: openers, follow-up, volume, tracking
- `cold-calls`: dialing, openers, gatekeepers, voicemail, call scripts
- `booking-the-call`: turning a reply or a yes into a booked meeting, no-shows, confirmations
- `running-a-sales-call`: discovery, diagnosis, presenting, closing on the call
- `handling-objections`: stalls, price pushback, "send me info", "I need to think"
- `pricing-an-offer`: setting or changing a price, price ladders, discounts, payment terms
- `building-an-offer`: what is in it, value framing, bonuses, guarantees, naming
- `choosing-a-niche-and-focus`: who to serve, what to say no to, narrowing
- `launching-a-product`: getting a new offer or product out the door, launch sequences
- `content-cadence`: how often to post, batching, volume, consistency
- `content-ideas-and-formats`: what to make, formats, angles, repurposing
- `hooks-and-editing`: openings, captions, cuts, pacing, retention (hooks.md and editing.md hold the weekly-researched view)
- `measuring-content`: which numbers to read, what a good post looks like in data
- `turning-followers-into-buyers`: moving an audience to a sale, CTAs, warm-audience offers
- `paid-ads`: spending on ads, creative, targeting, when to start
- `email-list`: building and using an email list, newsletters, sequences
- `landing-pages`: page structure, above the fold, conversion testing
- `community-and-membership`: running a paid community or membership, engagement, churn
- `retention-and-upsell`: keeping customers paying, upsells, downsells, continuity
- `hiring-and-delegating`: who to hire, when, handing work off, managing people
- `time-and-focus`: what to work on, priorities, energy, saying no
- `ai-agents-and-automation`: AI tools, agents, automations and workflows that run the work
- `storytelling`: story structure, anecdotes, narrative in content and sales
- `positioning-and-brand`: how you are seen, differentiation, brand, authority
- `difficult-people-and-politics`: dealing with a manipulative boss, client or partner, office politics and egos
